Partnering is working together on an outcome. Advisory, board service, and startup support -- scoped around what needs to be true at the end, not hours at a desk.

What partnering looks like

Advisory engagements are scoped around an outcome: judgment, process, deliverables, and a defined end state -- working systems, not a timesheet. Board service is governance and oversight: risk, AI governance, strategy to execution, independent judgment. Startup support is for teams bringing AI into industrial settings: the engineering discipline that keeps plants safe, applied to tools that calculate while you decide.

Who it is for

Founders and teams building AI for physical operations. Boards that want an independent engineering voice in the room. If you run physical infrastructure -- plants, grids, industrial operations -- and you are bringing AI into decisions where failure has physical consequences, this is for you.

A clear boundary

I don't take consulting engagements with oil and gas companies. I work in the industry, and keeping clear of conflicts of interest matters more than any single engagement.
