Privacy

A personal note, in plain words — last updated October 28, 2026
I built this site respecting my own privacy — no trackers watching over it, no data business behind it — and I extend the same respect to you. This page says what the site collects, why, and what happens to it. It describes this site as it actually runs, not a policy template.
Whose site this is
nrupalakolkar.com is my personal website — a private blog and news space. It is a place for my own development, and for information I share personally: my writing, my projects, my professional record. It carries a commercial angle — my book, and the advisory work described on the consulting page — but it is a personal site, written and run by me, Nrupal Akolkar, in Calgary. There is no marketing team and no data operation behind it.
The name in the footer — 2351485 ALBERTA INC. — is my company, and this site is published under it. Everything on it is written by me personally.
What the forms collect
Reading this site asks nothing of you. The only information I receive is what you type into a form:
Contact form
Your name, your email address, the topic you pick, your message — and any files you choose to attach.
Launch-notify form (books page)
Your email address, so I can write to you once when the book is out.
Book order form
Your name, email address, and postal address, plus the format and quantity — what I need to get the book to you.
When a form is submitted, the site also records the time, and the internet address the submission came from. That is there to spot spam and abuse; the contact form runs anti-spam checks for the same reason.
What happens to it
Every submission is stored in a private log and forwarded to my own inbox. I read them myself. I use what you send for exactly one thing: to answer you, or to deliver what you asked for — a reply, a launch note, a book. Files you attach are stored with your message, in the same private log.
I do not sell, rent, or trade your information. I do not share it with advertisers or data brokers — there are none in this site's life.
Two services carry the mechanics: the site is hosted on Cloudflare, and form entries pass through a private spreadsheet in my own Google account on their way to my inbox. They process submissions only so the site works.
What the site does not do
The site's own code sets no cookies and runs no advertising trackers, and there are no user accounts. The read-aloud feature on some pages works entirely inside your browser — when you listen to a page, nothing is sent anywhere.
Your browser keeps a few preferences on your own device: the colour theme you pick, whether the banner at the top is open, and where you stopped in a read-aloud. Those stay on your device; they never reach me.
Some pages load one script from Google, for Google's preferred-source and subscribe features. What Google does on its side of that script is governed by Google's own privacy policy.
Your say over what you sent
If you want to know what you have sent me, or you want it deleted, write through the contact form and say so — I will deal with it personally. One honest expectation: I read everything, and I try to reply to all of it, but not quickly. Expect to wait at least three to four weeks.
Changes to this note
If this note changes, the new version will be posted on this page, with its date. The version you are reading is dated October 28, 2026.

Questions about any of this? The contact form reaches me directly.
