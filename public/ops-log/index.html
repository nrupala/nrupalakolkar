How this board is kept

  • A row is written the day the act happens — never after.
  • A status changes only on live-surface verification. An email earns at most “in motion”; “live” requires the surface itself.
  • Every row carries its last-verified date; stale rows are re-verified before they are cited anywhere.
  • When trackers disagree, the one with the fresher direct verification wins — and the other is corrected the same day.

Worked example, 2026-10-22: a fleet sweep reported the Smithery listings absent and a Registry version that does not exist. Direct reads at the source the same day proved the listings live (LetsAskIt, Conduit, codetopo) and the Registry holding exactly one LetsAskIt record, v0.4.0. Every tracker was corrected that day, with the evidence attached. The log exists so that class of miss cannot hide again.
