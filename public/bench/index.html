Open distillation candidates

  1. Nightly self-test re-verification. Proposed local form: one nightly-selftest runner executing the registered checks against their founding inputs and emitting the verdict table the nightly review currently assembles by hand.
  2. Tailnet SSH jump procedure. Proposed local form: fleet-ssh.sh <host-alias> wrapping the verified recipe, refusing on fingerprint mismatch.
  3. Cron job-body gate carriage. Proposed local form: cron-body-check.py scanning job bodies for the required gate markers — the deploy-brief-check.py pattern applied to crons.
  4. Gate-verdict rollup. Proposed local form: verdict-rollup.py reading runner JSON (and, until the prose packs converge, a mandated first-line verdict) into the M6 series automatically.
  5. Registry-vs-live drift check. Proposed local form: a nightly diff of registry status claims against live cron/tool state, flagging mismatches instead of discovering them by rereading.
