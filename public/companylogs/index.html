Coverage note and evidence

This log was compiled from three evidence sources, in order of weight:

  1. GitHub — repository creation dates, release and tag publication dates for the account's repositories (169 repositories enumerated on 2026-10-12). A repository creation date is evidence of the repository, not necessarily of the product's first existence; where a product predates its repository, the entry says so rather than backdating.
  2. Working records — daily logs and the curated memory file covering 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-12 in detail (decisions, submissions, launches, and their dates are recorded there contemporaneously).
  3. Workspace records — project directories, goal files, and research notes.

Entries whose date is directly evidenced are stated plainly.

Effort and costs methodology (added 2026-10-12, at the owner's instruction). Bills and other recorded amounts are matched to products in the roster appendix ("Costs matched"). Only amounts actually recorded in the working records are entered, each with its date; where a product has known infrastructure but no recorded amount, the entry reads "Not recorded" — amounts are never invented, and credits or funding applications are labeled as such, never as cash or as costs. Estimated hours ("Effort (est. hours)" in the roster; detail in ~/workspace/corporate/effort-ledger.md) were built two ways: (a) for 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-12, from the density of work recorded in the contemporaneous daily logs, per product, in round numbers; (b) for earlier periods, from GitHub commit volume per repository at a conservative conversion (0.5 h per commit, floored at 5 h and capped at 50 h per product), rounded down to the nearest 5 h — these earlier figures are lower confidence and are labeled "(low confidence)" in the effort ledger. Hours measure combined founder and directed build-session effort, are estimates throughout, and understate rather than overstate wherever the evidence allowed a range. Entries whose date is reconstructed or approximate are marked (date inferred). Coverage before September 2026 rests mainly on GitHub dates and is therefore thinner: it records what was created, and only those decisions that left a dated trace. Personal learning repositories and third-party forks are excluded; this log covers the corporation's products, websites, and the decisions that govern them.
