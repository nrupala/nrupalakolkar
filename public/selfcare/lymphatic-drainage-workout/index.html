Lymphatic Drainage
a 10-minute gentle routine

Ten slow, gentle exercises — neck to belly, standing to lying. No equipment, no impact. Just press start and follow along.

How it works

10exercises
50sgentle movement
10srest between

Move slowly and stay pain-free. Breathe easy throughout — the last exercise is deep belly breathing to finish.

A few kind notes

  • Start gently and build up over days and weeks — never push through pain or swelling.
  • Exercises 1–5 are standing (or seated); 6–10 are lying down. Use the rest before #6 to get on the floor.
  • For personal guidance, talk to your healthcare provider or a lymphedema therapist.
