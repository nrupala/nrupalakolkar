Seven standing movements that move fluid from head to toe — breathing, twists, calf pumps, and a finishing bounce. No equipment, no impact. Press start and follow along.

Move slowly and stay pain-free. Work from the top down toward the main drains — the last minute is a full-body bounce to flush the system.

A few kind notes

  • All seven movements are standing — clear a little floor space and keep knees soft.
  • Start gently and build up over days and weeks — never push through pain or swelling.
  • For personal guidance, talk to your healthcare provider or a lymphedema therapist.
