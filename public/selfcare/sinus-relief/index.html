For congested days

Sinus Pressure relief

Sinus congestion jams the neck, back, chest and diaphragm. This 5-step sequence opens the main lymphatic drains behind your collarbones first — so fluid from your head and chest has somewhere to go — then releases the pressure step by step.

Do it in order

    Two things that help

    • Hydrate. Lymphatic fluid is mostly water — it can't flow if you're dehydrated.
    • Use gravity. Don't lie flat right after; stay upright or propped on pillows so drainage keeps moving down and out.

    General wellness information, not medical advice. Use very light pressure throughout — if anything hurts, stop. Talk to your healthcare provider for personal guidance.
