For congested days

Sinus Pressure relief

Sinus congestion tightens the neck, back, chest and diaphragm. This 5-step sequence starts with gentle touch around your collarbones, then loosens the neck, chest and breathing step by step.

The sequence

Tap any step to do just that one — or run the full sequence below.

    Two things that help

    • Hydrate. Drink some water — moving feels better when you're hydrated.
    • Use gravity. Don't lie flat right after; stay upright or propped on pillows for a while.

    General wellness information, not medical advice. Use very light pressure throughout — if anything hurts, stop. Talk to your healthcare provider for personal guidance.
