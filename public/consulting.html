Who this is for

If you run physical infrastructure — plants, grids, industrial operations — and you're bringing AI into decisions where failure has physical consequences, this page is for you.
The hard part isn't the model. It's knowing what the model doesn't know about your plant.

A clear boundary

I don't take consulting engagements with oil and gas companies. I work in the industry, and keeping clear of conflicts of interest matters more than any single engagement. Power, utilities, manufacturing, and infrastructure — everything outside oil and gas — is in scope.

What I help with

AI for industrial operations
Applying AI to maintenance, reliability, and operations — without losing the engineering discipline that keeps plants safe. The tool calculates, you decide.
Power systems engineering
Substations, protection coordination, arc flash, power distribution. Design review, troubleshooting, and an owner's-engineering perspective.
Analytics & decision support
Turning operating data into decisions: models you can audit, dashboards that matter, numbers you can defend.
Operational excellence
Lean Six Sigma, reliability strategy, process improvement. The unglamorous work that moves the needle.

How I work

I prefer to take only a few engagements at a time. Each one is scoped around an outcome, not hours — you get judgment, process, deliverables, and a defined outcome: working systems, not a timesheet. I work remotely and independently.
I also occasionally advise AI teams building for industrial settings.

Background, briefly

24+ years in safety-critical energy systems — oil sands production operations, thermal power plants, substations and grids. P.Eng., MBA in Operations. Since 2020 I've built open-source, local-first AI tools to institutional standards — because the discipline of systems that can't fail is exactly what AI needs.

Two doors

I've done pro bono work in the past, and I'll still take on the occasional pro bono engagement where the need is real.
