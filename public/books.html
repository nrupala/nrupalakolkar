Outcome Convergence Systems
A book by Nrupal Akolkar, P.Eng.
How systems converge on outcomes -- the mathematics of feedback, constraints, and control, built up from first principles and grounded in two worked examples: predictive maintenance and markets.
What is inside
  • The transformation operator and the closed loop
  • Feedback as the engine of convergence
  • When convergence is guaranteed: contraction and Lyapunov
  • Constraints and feasible convergence
  • Observers and acting under partial information
  • Goodhart, proxies, and how good loops break
  • Worked example: predictive maintenance
  • Worked example: convergence in markets
