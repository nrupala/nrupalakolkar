# Blog voice guardrails

The bar every post on nrupalakolkar.com/blog is measured against before
publishing. This is a personal identity site: the blog carries Nrupal's
voice — the same voice as the resume, the board bio, and LinkedIn. One
converged voice, not separate realms.

## The language bar

> "We never want to feel cheap or slangy — least words most meaning —
> clarity is king."

Calm, exact, economical writing. Short declarative sentences. Concrete
nouns and active verbs. No hype, no slang, no cute phrasing, no chatty
assistant language.

## Public-copy doctrine

- Poised, subtle, inviting, non-salesy.
- State process and outcomes, not self. Confident on process, modest
  on self.
- No negatives about others; no strong claims ("nobody else knows",
  "only I can deliver" — presumptuous).
- "Never" is a banned word in public copy.
- Name industries, not employers.
- Hands off self: what gets done, what results — the work is the
  subject, not the author.

## Board-bio lessons

- Lead with judgment. A few signature outcomes as evidence.
- The resume, the board bio, LinkedIn, and the blog converge into one
  voice. A reader of any one should recognize the same person in the
  others.

## Standing facts (do not drift)

- Muse is one of the AI tools I build with — never the only one.
- 24+ years in safety-critical energy systems.
- Title: Independent Open-Source Builder.
- Book: Outcome Convergence Systems.
- No ads, no sale of user data.

## The gate

Every post is measured against this document before publishing. If a
sentence feels cheap, slangy, or salesy, it is rewritten or cut.
